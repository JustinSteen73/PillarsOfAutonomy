Knowledge Base

Welcome to the Harper AIC110 knowledge base! This knowledge base was built by Justin at PillarOfAutonomy for Harper College's AIC110 course (Introduction to Artificial Intelligence). Here you will find information about Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning.

Artifical Intelligence (AI) - The development of computer systems able to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
Machine Learning (ML) - A subset of AI which uses statistical methods to enable machines to improve with experiences.
Deep Learning - A subset of Machine Learning that makes the computation of neural networks feasible.
  • Supervised Learning - Patterns and relationships learned through training
